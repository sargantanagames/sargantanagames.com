VPETLINGS PRESS KIT
====================

SHORT DESCRIPTION
-----------------

VPetlings is a cozy virtual pet that lives on your desktop. Hatch a little
companion, care for them, play together, decorate their room, and raise them
across generations as they evolve and train for battle.

ABOUT THE GAME
--------------

VPetlings is a desktop virtual pet game about raising a small creature from an
egg. Each companion wanders around the desktop, eats, sleeps, interacts with
the cursor, plays, and develops its own moods, stats, and sleep patterns.
Players can care for their pet throughout the day or send them to rest in
their room when they need to focus.

Pets gain experience and evolve over time. At the end of a generation, they
can be reborn into a new egg with a head start toward discovering more
creatures. Players can decorate their room, unlock color variants, train their
stats, explore small worlds, and take part in timing-based battles. VPetlings
combines the care of Tamagotchi, the evolutions and battles of Digimon V-Pets,
and the charm of old desktop pets.

FEATURES
--------

- A virtual pet that roams, rests, and plays directly on your desktop
- Care, play, room decoration, color variants, and distinct personalities
- Multiple generations of creatures to evolve and discover
- Stats, training, small worlds, and timing-based battles
- Designed to provide company while working, playing, or browsing
